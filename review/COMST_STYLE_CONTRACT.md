# IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials Style Contract

## Target form

The eventual article is prepared as a **Survey** unless the frozen evidence package clearly requires reclassification.

The manuscript must be useful to a communications/networking generalist while remaining technically rigorous for MANET specialists.

## Mandatory COMST-facing principles

1. **Survey identity and scope**
   - State explicitly in the abstract that the article is a survey.
   - State the survey scope explicitly in both abstract and introduction.
   - Define the literature period covered after the final search freeze.

2. **Developmental narrative**
   - Cover the selected MANET-routing area from foundational development through the current state and future research obligations.
   - Explain why each major mechanism emerged, what limitation it addressed, what cost it introduced, and what remained unresolved.
   - Do not present a chronological catalogue without causal synthesis.

3. **Tutorial readability**
   - Define specialist terminology before use.
   - Make each taxonomy dimension interpretable to a communications researcher outside MANET routing.
   - Prefer explanatory diagrams over unnecessary formalism.
   - Use equations only where they materially clarify comparability, applicability, uncertainty, or failure/frontier concepts.

4. **Technical depth**
   - Preserve implementation identity, evaluation conditions, evidence maturity, uncertainty, reproducibility, and limitations.
   - Distinguish reported evidence from controlled Observatory evidence.
   - Never collapse heterogeneous published results into an unsupported leaderboard.

5. **Non-redundancy**
   - Compare explicitly against the closest recent surveys.
   - Every claimed contribution must have a documented delta from prior survey coverage.
   - Avoid unsupported priority language such as first, only, or most comprehensive.

6. **Figures and tables**
   - Use high-information figures and tables liberally.
   - Every figure/table answers a research question or supports reader navigation.
   - Keep main-paper tables compressed and readable at double-column scale.
   - Move exhaustive machine-readable records to the repository/supplement.

7. **References**
   - Comprehensive for survey claims.
   - Prefer primary specifications/original works for protocol mechanisms.
   - Use verified references only for substantive claims.
   - Number references sequentially in the manuscript.
   - Retain contradictory and negative evidence.

8. **Page discipline**
   - Design the initial submission for a maximum of 30 double-column pages under the current COMST rule.
   - Treat page budget as an architectural constraint, not a late formatting exercise.

## Writing style

Preferred paragraph structure:
**context/problem -> mechanism/evidence -> qualification -> implication/transition**

Preferred comparative statement:
**Under [condition], the available evidence indicates [effect], subject to [limitation/uncertainty].**

Avoid:
- marketing language;
- universal winner claims;
- unsupported causal language;
- decorative equations;
- giant protocol inventories in prose;
- repetition of table contents.

## Abstract construction

Write the abstract after the results and conclusion are frozen.

It must contain:
- article category: Survey;
- explicit scope and literature period;
- problem motivating the survey;
- organizing evidence framework;
- principal synthesis findings;
- role of controlled reproducibility evidence;
- evidence-backed research obligations;
- no claim absent from the paper body.

## Introduction construction

The introduction must establish:
1. why MANET routing still requires synthesis;
2. why existing surveys do not fully resolve the selected evidence problem;
3. exact scope and exclusions;
4. survey period;
5. research questions;
6. contributions mapped to paper outputs;
7. organization of the article.

## Final COMST audit

Before submission:
- category/scope present in abstract and introduction;
- literature current through freeze date;
- closest surveys explicitly differentiated;
- all technical statements checked;
- tutorial value visible;
- figures/tables readable;
- references comprehensive and verified;
- initial manuscript <=30 double-column pages under current guidance;
- current COMST policies rechecked immediately before submission.

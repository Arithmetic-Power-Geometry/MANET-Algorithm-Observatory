# Evidence Maturity Ladder

Evidence maturity is reported independently from mechanism novelty, verification, and comparability.

- **E0 — Definition:** mechanism or specification exists.
- **E1 — Demonstration:** limited illustrative simulation or analytical example.
- **E2 — Comparative simulation:** comparison against meaningful baselines, with potentially limited scenario or statistical breadth.
- **E3 — Reproducible comparison:** implementation, configuration, seeds or data, and analysis are sufficient for reproduction.
- **E4 — Robustness evidence:** deliberate sensitivity across relevant nuisance or operating conditions.
- **E5 — Generalization / stress evidence:** held-out or out-of-distribution conditions, including adversarial or failure stress where relevant.
- **E6 — Emulation / testbed evidence:** physical or high-fidelity testbed evaluation.
- **E7 — Field evidence:** operational mobile deployment evidence.
- **E8 — Independent replication:** important claims reproduced by an independent group or implementation.

These levels are evidence descriptors rather than quality scores or protocol rankings. A mechanism may be theoretically or architecturally sophisticated while still having limited empirical maturity.

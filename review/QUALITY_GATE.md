# Foundation Review Quality Gate

The review is not considered ready for drafting until all applicable gates pass.

## Coverage
- Foundational classical protocols traced to primary sources.
- Standards status verified.
- Major routing branches represented.
- Security, energy, QoS, optimization, learning, prediction, cross-layer, and deployment evidence represented.
- Search is current through the frozen review date.

## Evidence
- Every major factual claim traceable to a source row.
- Performance claims retain scenario context.
- Missing experimental details remain missing rather than inferred.
- Contradictory evidence is retained.
- Review conclusions distinguish published evidence from Observatory-controlled evidence.

## Comparability
- No heterogeneous literature values presented as a common leaderboard.
- C0–C4 class assigned to quantitative comparisons used in synthesis.
- Propagation, mobility, traffic, scale, and metric-definition differences audited.

## Reproducibility
- Code/implementation status verified where claimed.
- Seeds/repetitions/statistics recorded.
- Simulator/version/configuration captured where available.
- Learning hyperparameters/training setup captured for learning methods.

## Novelty
- Closest recent reviews explicitly compared.
- Each claimed review contribution has a documented delta.
- No unsupported "first", "only", or "most comprehensive" language.

## Experimental bridge
- Benchmark admission criteria frozen.
- Candidate algorithms selected by scientific relevance and implementation validity, not convenience.
- Development and confirmatory scenarios separated.
- Failure/frontier hypotheses preregistered in repository files before confirmatory runs.

## Presentation
- Every flagship figure answers one RQ.
- Main tables are readable without repository access.
- Complete status data remain downloadable/machine-readable.
- Timeline is causal, not merely chronological.
- Gap map distinguishes absence of literature from demonstrated capability failure.

## Drafting gate
Paper prose begins only after the review corpus, status atlas, closest-review matrix, and first generated evidence artifacts are frozen.

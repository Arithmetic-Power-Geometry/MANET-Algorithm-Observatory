# Paper Writing Trigger

## Full paper drafting begins when

- P0 literature tasks are complete or scientifically waived;
- all substantive synthesis claims are supported by V2+ sources;
- synthesis evidence is extracted at V3 where required;
- closest-review matrix is updated through the final search date;
- Tier-1 and any admitted expanded benchmark runs are complete;
- metric definitions, seeds and scenarios are frozen;
- statistical and robustness analyses are complete;
- ranking-stability/failure-frontier analyses are complete where supported;
- gap claims distinguish literature gaps from demonstrated capability gaps;
- the novel-algorithm gate is resolved;
- final paper figures/tables regenerate from versioned data;
- Streamlit evidence views correspond to the same frozen evidence release;
- a clean reproduction run passes;
- repository release/tag is frozen;
- journal decision gate is evaluated against current official guidance.

## Drafting order after the gate opens

1. Methods / review protocol
2. Taxonomy and evidence model
3. Verified results and artifact descriptions
4. Comparative synthesis and frontier analysis
5. Research obligations / discussion
6. Threats to validity
7. Introduction and contribution statement
8. Conclusion
9. Abstract
10. Final title

Writing the abstract last prevents the paper from promising results that the completed evidence does not support.

# COMST Evidence-to-Claim Gate

A substantive comparative sentence may enter the manuscript only if:

1. the source/result has immutable provenance;
2. the metric is admissible under the metric contract;
3. the implementation identity is known;
4. the operating condition is explicit;
5. independent replication is adequate for the stated inference;
6. uncertainty is reported when the claim is quantitative;
7. relevant implementation limitations are disclosed;
8. the sentence does not generalize beyond the evidence level;
9. contradictory evidence has been checked;
10. the claim maps to a table, figure, evidence row, or primary source.

## Claim levels

L0 — definition/specification claim  
L1 — individual-study reported claim  
L2 — cross-study synthesis claim  
L3 — controlled Observatory empirical claim  
L4 — robustness/frontier claim  
L5 — capability-gap claim

Higher levels require all lower-level provenance plus the additional evidence appropriate to the inference.

## Paper rule

No sentence is upgraded from L3 to L4 merely because an effect is statistically significant. Robustness requires deliberate variation of relevant nuisance/operating conditions.

No sentence is upgraded to L5 merely because literature coverage is sparse. A capability-gap claim requires controlled evidence that the relevant capability remains unresolved under the stated condition.

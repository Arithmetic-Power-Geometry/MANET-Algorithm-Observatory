# Paper Flow Map

## Reader journey

**Why another review?**
↓
**How was the evidence assembled and verified?**
↓
**Why did routing families evolve?**
↓
**How can all mechanisms be described using common dimensions?**
↓
**What is the current status of each family?**
↓
**How mature and reproducible is the evidence?**
↓
**Why do published conclusions disagree?**
↓
**What happens under a controlled common benchmark?**
↓
**Where do rankings change and algorithms fail requirements?**
↓
**Which gaps survive both literature synthesis and controlled testing?**
↓
**How should the next MANET routing study be designed?**

## Argument discipline

Each section must create the need for the next section.

- Evolution motivates taxonomy.
- Taxonomy makes status comparison possible.
- Status exposes evidence heterogeneity.
- Evidence heterogeneity motivates comparability analysis.
- Comparability limitations motivate controlled benchmarking.
- Benchmark results enable frontier analysis.
- Frontiers distinguish speculative gaps from capability gaps.
- Capability gaps support a falsifiable research agenda.

This dependency chain is the preferred arrangement because it prevents disconnected survey sections.

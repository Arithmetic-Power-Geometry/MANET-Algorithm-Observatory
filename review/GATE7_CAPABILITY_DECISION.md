# Gate 7 — Capability-gap decision

## Decision
**NO-GO for a new adaptive/AI routing algorithm in the present paper.**

The verified primary corpus already contains geographic routing, multipath/metaheuristic routing, Q-learning adaptation, predictive/adaptive routing, deep reinforcement learning, federated trust-aware routing, and MARL+GNN dynamic MANET routing. Therefore, novelty cannot be defended merely by combining adaptation, prediction, learning, graph representation, trust, energy, or multiple objectives.

## Evidence-backed gap that survives
The surviving research problem is **comparative evidence validity** rather than absence of an algorithmic family:

1. Published routing claims are often generated under different simulators, propagation assumptions, mobility/load regimes, seeds, metrics, and baseline sets.
2. Controlled external evidence (M0012) shows propagation assumptions can alter comparative conclusions.
3. Observatory S2 provides 320 frozen common-contract runs and shows substantial seed/scenario-dependent ranking changes among four classical implementations.
4. Recent learning literature contains increasingly sophisticated adaptive methods, but the current verified corpus does not establish that these heterogeneous methods have been compared under one condition-indexed, implementation-provenance-aware, uncertainty-aware MANET evidence contract.
5. This is a **review/benchmark/evidence-system gap**, not yet proof of an absent routing capability.

## G-CAP status
G005 remains **NOT ESTABLISHED** as an algorithmic capability gap. P019 is therefore waived/no-go for this paper. No novel routing algorithm will be introduced merely to create a method contribution.

## Paper contribution direction
The paper should be framed as a reproducible MANET routing evidence atlas: historical mechanism evolution; condition-indexed comparability; reproducibility/maturity; controlled classical benchmark; ranking instability; claim limits; and evidence-backed research obligations for future intelligent routing.

## Claim boundary
Do not claim that no previous survey or benchmark has ever combined these elements until the final closest-review search is frozen. Use “in the verified corpus” language until Gate 8.

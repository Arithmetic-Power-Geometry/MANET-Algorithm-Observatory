# Manuscript Evidence Contract

Evidence base: ERC-2026-10-01.

## Central paper question
Under what documented conditions can MANET routing evidence be compared, reproduced, and generalized, and what research obligations remain after condition-indexed synthesis and a controlled classical benchmark?

## Admitted principal contributions
1. A mechanism-centered evolution from classical routing to contemporary intelligent routing, grounded in verified primary and review evidence.
2. A common evidence model separating mechanism definition, study-local comparison, controlled comparison, robustness/generalization, and capability-gap claims.
3. A comparability/maturity atlas that prevents heterogeneous published values from becoming a false leaderboard.
4. A controlled ns-3.47 bridge across AODV, DSDV, DSR and OLSRv1 using four frozen scenarios and 20 predeclared seeds per protocol/scenario.
5. Evidence that seed/scenario variation can change rank membership; therefore the controlled results do not support a universal protocol winner.
6. A contradiction-resolution framework that reconstructs apparently conflicting results as condition-indexed claims where appropriate.
7. Evidence-backed research obligations for reproducibility, generalization, resource-cost accounting, deployment validation and capability-first novelty.

## Prohibited manuscript claims
- first/only/most comprehensive unless independently established;
- universal best MANET routing protocol;
- continuous failure frontier from the four S2 scenarios;
- Observatory propagation-sensitivity result;
- routing-cost Pareto frontier without validated cost measurements;
- field superiority inferred from simulation;
- absent intelligent-routing capability: verified literature already contains RL, DRL, federated, MARL and GNN approaches;
- quantitative cross-paper leaderboard from C0-C2 evidence.

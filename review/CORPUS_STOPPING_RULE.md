# Corpus stopping rule

Frozen review date: 2026-10-01.

The Observatory uses conceptual saturation rather than an impossible claim of exhaustive enumeration. A routing family is considered represented for synthesis when (1) its defining or representative mechanism is anchored in at least one verified primary/specification source where available, (2) recent review/meta-evidence is used to check whether major mechanism branches have been omitted, and (3) any quantitative claim used in synthesis is extracted at the evidence level required by that claim. Family representation does not imply that every publication has been collected.

Search continues when a source reveals a previously unrepresented decision mechanism, information scope, temporal mode, deployment regime, or materially conflicting result. Search stops when additional sources only repeat already represented mechanism classes without changing the synthesis. Missing details remain missing; they are never inferred.

Adjacent VANET/FANET/DTN/IoT evidence is retained only as transfer or boundary evidence unless the study directly includes the MANET population. The final manuscript must state this boundary and the frozen search date.

This stopping rule supports a structured evidence atlas; it does not support claims such as “all MANET papers,” “the first ever,” or “the only comprehensive review.”

# Corpus Stopping Rule

The review uses conceptual saturation rather than exhaustive enumeration.

A routing family is considered represented for synthesis when:

1. its defining or representative mechanism is anchored in verified primary or specification evidence where available;
2. recent review or meta-evidence has been used to check for omitted major mechanism branches;
3. contradictory or negative evidence has been considered;
4. each quantitative synthesis claim has been extracted at the evidence level required by that claim.

Search continues when a source reveals a previously unrepresented decision mechanism, information scope, temporal mode, deployment regime, or materially conflicting result. Search stops when additional sources repeat already represented mechanism classes without changing the synthesis. Missing experimental details remain missing and are not inferred.

Adjacent VANET, FANET, DTN, mesh, WSN, IoT, and related studies are retained only as transfer or boundary evidence unless they directly include the MANET population.

This stopping rule supports a structured evidence synthesis and does not support claims such as “all MANET papers,” “first ever,” or “only comprehensive review.”

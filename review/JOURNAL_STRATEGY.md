# Journal Strategy

Verified: 2026-10-01. Journal metrics and quartiles are intentionally not hard-coded here because they change; verify them at submission.

## Primary aspirational target — IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials

### Why the project can fit
The journal explicitly expects surveys to:
- comprehensively cover development from inception to the current state and beyond;
- be tutorial in nature and understandable outside the immediate specialty;
- use figures and tables liberally;
- provide comprehensive references;
- be timely, useful, and non-redundant.

The new-submission length constraint is especially important: no more than 30 pages in double-column format; revised manuscripts should remain below 40 pages.

### Consequence for this project
The repository may contain the complete evidence atlas, but the paper must be a carefully compressed navigation system. Large raw status tables belong in machine-readable artifacts/supplementary material rather than the main narrative.

### COMST-level acceptance obligations
- Demonstrate non-redundancy against the closest surveys.
- Teach the field, not merely enumerate papers.
- Make the taxonomy explanatory.
- Keep mathematics only where it clarifies a new evidence concept.
- Use high-information figures/tables.
- Make the research agenda arise from evidence, not generic future-work prose.

## Strong scope-aligned target — Ad Hoc Networks

The journal explicitly covers mobile/wireless ad hoc networks, routing, energy, mobility, security/fault tolerance, hardware/software platforms and testbeds, QoS, cross-layer interactions, scalability, and performance analysis/simulation.

This is the most natural domain-specific target if the final contribution emphasizes the MANET Observatory, experimental methodology, and controlled benchmark.

## Additional candidates

### Computer Networks
Strong fit for routing protocols, performance evaluation, wireless/mobile networking, network management, security, and algorithmic networking. Particularly suitable if the controlled benchmark becomes a major contribution.

### Computer Communications
Publishes survey papers and covers future computer communication networks, architectures, protocols, services, and applications. Suitable fallback for a broad networking survey with strong methodology.

### Journal of Network and Computer Applications
Publishes substantial review articles in networking/application areas. More attractive if intelligent routing, deployability, and application implications dominate the final paper.

## Submission decision gate

Do not choose the final journal from prestige alone. After artifacts are complete, evaluate:
1. breadth versus specialist depth;
2. tutorial value;
3. controlled experimental contribution;
4. final page count;
5. novelty relative to recent surveys;
6. current journal scope and article type;
7. current quartile/metrics from an authoritative source available to the authors.

## Current strategy

Build to the most demanding survey architecture first. Compress for the selected venue only after the evidence and artifacts are frozen.

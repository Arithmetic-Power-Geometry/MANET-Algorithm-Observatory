# Review-Level Novelty Framework

The intended contribution is not "more papers summarized." The review is designed to change how MANET routing evidence is organized and used.

## N1 — One ontology from classical protocols to learning systems
Describe all routing approaches through common decision primitives:
- decision role;
- information scope;
- temporal horizon;
- state;
- control mechanism;
- compute;
- adaptation;
- cost.

## N2 — Claim-conditioned evidence
Performance claims remain attached to the conditions that produced them.

## N3 — Comparability as an explicit research object
C0–C4 comparability prevents invalid cross-paper leaderboards.

## N4 — Evidence maturity separated from algorithm novelty
Simulation volume is not treated as deployment maturity.

## N5 — Information and cost fairness
Comparisons disclose whether a method wins partly by observing more state, using more communication, or consuming more compute.

## N6 — Contradiction resolution
Conflicting claims are classified as:
- genuine contradiction;
- scenario-conditioned difference;
- metric-definition mismatch;
- implementation difference;
- unresolved.

## N7 — Failure/frontier view
The review asks where a method stops satisfying requirements, not only where it achieves its best mean.

## N8 — Gap hierarchy
Literature absence is separated from reproducibility, evaluation, robustness, generalization, cost, real-world, contradiction, and capability gaps.

## N9 — Executable bridge
The review motivates a shared benchmark; the benchmark later tests claims without mixing its results with literature evidence.

## N10 — Research obligations
Future-work statements are rewritten as falsifiable obligations:
- missing comparison;
- missing environment;
- missing stress test;
- missing cost;
- missing deployment;
- unresolved contradiction.

## N11 — Living machine-readable status
The complete evidence/status atlas can evolve independently of the static paper.

## N12 — Researcher navigation
The final presentation supports entry by problem, decision, mechanism, evidence maturity, application condition, or unresolved obligation.

## Novelty claim discipline

The final manuscript should claim these contributions only after the closest-review matrix is complete. It should avoid unsupported priority language.

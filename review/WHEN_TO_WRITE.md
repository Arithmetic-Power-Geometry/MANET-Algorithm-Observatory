# When the Paper Is Written

## Principle

The manuscript is the final scholarly synthesis of a completed evidence package. It is not the workspace in which the evidence is invented.

## Writing trigger

Begin full drafting only after all of the following are true:

1. **Corpus freeze** — all major routing families have reached evidence saturation under the review protocol.
2. **Evidence freeze** — synthesis studies have V3 structured evidence and substantive claims are traceable to verified sources.
3. **Non-redundancy freeze** — closest contemporary reviews have been checked through the final search date.
4. **Benchmark freeze** — implementation provenance, scenarios, seeds, metrics and fairness rules are fixed.
5. **Experiment completion** — controlled benchmark and required robustness/sensitivity runs are complete.
6. **Inference completion** — uncertainty, statistical comparisons, ranking stability and failure-frontier analyses are complete.
7. **Gap decision** — G-LIT/G-REP/G-EVAL/G-CONTRA/G-ROB/G-GEN/G-COST/G-REAL are separated from demonstrated G-CAP.
8. **Novel-algorithm decision** — a new algorithm is included only if a verified capability gap justifies it and its confirmatory evidence is complete.
9. **Artifact freeze** — final figures, tables and algorithm blocks regenerate from versioned data.
10. **Reproducibility freeze** — clean-run regeneration passes and the evidence release is tagged/archived.
11. **Journal recheck** — current target-journal scope, article type and formatting constraints are verified immediately before drafting.

## What may be written before the trigger

Allowed:
- evidence notes;
- taxonomy definitions;
- figure/table specifications;
- captions as working notes;
- section architecture;
- review protocol;
- benchmark protocol.

Not yet allowed:
- final abstract;
- final results narrative;
- final discussion;
- novelty priority claims;
- final conclusion.

## Why

Writing results before evidence freeze encourages narrative lock-in and selective interpretation. The Observatory instead fixes the evidence first and lets the final narrative follow the verified results.

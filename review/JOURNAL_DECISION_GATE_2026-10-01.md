# Journal decision gate — frozen 2026-10-01

Target: IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials (COMST).

Official current guidance was rechecked on 2026-10-01. The paper fits listed scope areas including telecommunications switching/routing, wireless and mobile/ad hoc networks, machine learning for networking, and performance modeling/simulation/test-bed experimentation.

Submission constraints to enforce:
- classify the article explicitly as a **survey** in both abstract and introduction;
- state the survey scope and frozen review period explicitly;
- write tutorially for communications generalists;
- cover development from inception to current state and beyond with comprehensive references;
- use figures and tables liberally;
- keep equations only where vital;
- new manuscript maximum: 30 pages, double-column;
- revised manuscript: less than 40 pages;
- references numbered sequentially;
- exclusive/original submission;
- ORCID required for all authors.

Paper-specific editorial decision:
The controlled S2 benchmark is supporting synthesis evidence inside a survey, not a stand-alone experimental-paper claim. The manuscript must lead with historical/evidence synthesis and use the Observatory to test comparability and ranking stability. No universal-winner, continuous-failure-frontier, or unsupported first/only claim is admitted.

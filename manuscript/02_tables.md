# Section II Tables

## Table II-1. Evidence-control framework

| Dimension | Levels | Function in the survey | What it does not mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verification | V0 discovery; V1 metadata; V2 cited-role verified; V3 structured extraction; V4 synthesis/artifact | Controls whether a source can support a substantive statement | Higher V does not imply a better algorithm |
| Comparability | C0 definition/mechanism; C1 study-local; C2 partial; C3 strongly aligned; C4 controlled/matched | Controls whether quantitative evidence may be compared | Same metric name does not imply comparability |
| Maturity | E0 definition; E1 demonstration; E2 comparative simulation; E3 reproducible comparison; E4 robustness; E5 generalization/stress; E6 testbed; E7 field; E8 independent replication | Describes the empirical depth supporting a claim/family | It is not a scalar protocol ranking |

## Table II-2. Population policy

| Evidence population | Treatment | Quantitative pooling |
|---|---|---|
| MANET routing | Primary population | Only when comparability permits |
| VANET/FANET/WSN/DTN/mesh/IoT/tactical | Transfer or boundary evidence when relevant | Not automatically pooled with MANET |
| Observatory Tier-1 benchmark | Controlled evidence stratum | C4 within the frozen contract |

## Table II-3. Corpus stopping rule

A family is represented when a verified defining/representative primary source is present, recent review/meta-evidence has been checked for major omitted branches, and every quantitative claim used in synthesis has evidence extracted at the level required by that claim. Search remains open when new evidence changes the mechanism taxonomy, deployment boundary, condition signature, or contradiction map.
